How DeepGaze IIE Predicts Where People Look

DeepGaze IIE treats attention as a probability distribution over possible fixation locations—not as a binary object mask.

Visualize the Distribution

The probability distribution can be mapped to a heatmap: warmer colors represent higher predicted fixation density, and cooler colors represent lower predicted fixation density.

The colors are only a visualization of the distribution. They are not additional model outputs.

Interpretation Note

Higher predicted fixation density does not necessarily mean that a region is more important, preferred, understood, or relevant to a specific task. It only means that the model predicts that free-viewing fixations are more likely to occur there.
